Scope

This UAT pack proves that the platform is ready for operational pilot or production readiness review. It focuses on the permit and Emergency Travel Certificate scope currently agreed with Sierra Leone Immigration Department.

In scopeMust pass
Airport permit processingApplication capture, passport review, payment handoff, approval, issuance, PDF, QR verification.
NRA receipt paymentReceipt upload/camera capture, printed receipt number, duplicate receipt prevention, reconciliation.
WanGov/GovPayETC payment only, plus optional permit digital payment when credentials are enabled.
Emergency Travel CertificatePublic application, passport upload/MRZ fallback, save and continue, payment, HQ review, status/email.
Operations and oversightDashboards, expiry reports, audit logs, transactions, reconciliation, role-based read-only views.

Later-phase integrations such as live Interpol/watchlist, Timatic/IATA rules, biometric or ePassport chip verification remain plugin-ready but must be separately certified before production use.

UAT Entry Criteria

  • UAT environment is separate from development and production.
  • Latest database migrations run successfully.
  • Seed data exists for airports, desks, users, countries, nationalities, purposes, and points of entry.
  • SMTP or mail trap is configured.
  • WanGov/GovPay sandbox is configured for ETC payment tests.
  • Test passport images and test receipt images are approved for UAT use.
  • Real citizen data is not used unless explicitly approved by SLID policy.
  • Automated test baseline is green or known skips are accepted.

Security And OWASP

  • Public registration is disabled.
  • Staff routes require auth, verified email, active user, and staff access middleware.
  • Role/title authorization blocks direct URL access, not only hidden buttons.
  • Airport-scoped users cannot access another airport's applications, invoices, permits, receipts, or reports.
  • Payment Officer is restricted to payment screens.
  • Receipt uploads and passport uploads are private and file-type restricted.
  • NRA receipt number reuse is blocked.
  • WanGov/GovPay webhook secret, payload size, replay/idempotency, and mismatch checks pass.
  • ETC status tokens and permit verification codes are rate-limited.
  • Security headers and CSP are present; runtime version disclosure is suppressed.
  • Production uses APP_DEBUG=false, secure encrypted cookies, HTTPS, and expose_php=Off.

Exit Criteria

  • All P0 and P1 defects are closed and retested.
  • All P2 defects are fixed or accepted by the SLID owner.
  • Permit flow passes application, payment, approval, issuance, PDF, and QR verification.
  • ETC flow passes public application, WanGov/GovPay payment, HQ decision, and applicant status/email.
  • Role-based access passes for all staff titles.
  • NRA receipt duplicate prevention and payment reconciliation pass.
  • PDF samples are approved by SLID.
  • Email sender and templates are approved.
  • Backup/restore, monitoring, security, and data-protection sign-offs are complete.
